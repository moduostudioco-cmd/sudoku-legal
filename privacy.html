1. Summary

The App is a puzzle game that works on your device. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and your game progress stays on your device. The App shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects the advertising data described below. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

3. Ads and consent

The App shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads from Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions the App shows Google’s consent form so you can choose whether ads may be personalized. You can change your choice in the device’s Google settings (Settings › Google › Ads) and reset your advertising ID there.

4. Purchases

The App has no purchases.

5. Deleting your data

Your progress is stored only on your device. In the App open Settings › Delete My Data (or uninstall the App) to erase it. We hold no copy. Ad data held by Google can be managed in your Google account’s ad settings.

6. Sharing

We share data only with Google AdMob, which shows the ads. It processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

7. Security

Data sent to Google AdMob is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage is perfectly secure.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Use Delete My Data in the App for local data, or contact moduostudio.co@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

10. International transfers

Google may process data in countries other than your own, using safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Changes
